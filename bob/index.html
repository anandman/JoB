Betting strategy

Sessions are simulated hand by hand against a real depleting shoe with basic strategy, so a counting system's advantage is measured rather than assumed. Play never deviates from basic strategy, which is what a betting-only counter does; index plays would add roughly a fifth again to counting's value and are not modelled.
