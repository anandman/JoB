Bettor Edge

Casino math you can check at the machine, and a ledger for what it cost.

Jacks or Bettor

Video poker

Pay tables and returns for eight games, strategy cards derived from the pay table itself, a hand analyzer that prices all 32 holds, plus bankroll, risk-of-ruin and W-2G handpay math. Reno floor data included.

Bettor or Bust

Blackjack

Basic strategy derived from your table's exact rules rather than looked up, a hand analyzer ranking every action by expected value, a play mode that scores each decision against perfect play, and the house edge for any rule set you can dial in.

Color Up

Session ledger

One row per session: what went in, what came out, the tier credits, any handpays, and how long it took — which is the number a spreadsheet of totals cannot give you. Everything stays in your own browser, and nothing is sent anywhere.
